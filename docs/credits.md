# Beltbound asset credits

This page records third-party assets used in Beltbound or included in the project.

- **Tiny Warfare Sprites v1.0**. Art by Mythryl Studio. The optional purchased pack supplies the blue woodland engineer, textured isometric terrain, rocks, and additional art for the asset library. Its `LICENSE.txt` accompanies the purchased download and covers use in finished games, standalone redistribution restrictions, and restrictions on AI training and reference use. [Mythryl Studio](https://mythryl.com) · [Tiny Warfare pack](https://mythrylstudio.itch.io/tiny-warfare)
- **UI Pack - Pixel Adventure** by **Kenney**. This general UI collection in `assets/ui/` is released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. [Official pack and license](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack-pixel-adventure)
- **Tuffy Regular 1.1** by **Thatcher Ulrich**. Public domain font used in the editor palette and debug overlay. [Font source and license notice](../assets/fonts/README.md)

For each new asset, record its name, creator, where it appears in the game, and a link to the original license. Keep any required attribution wording with the entry.
