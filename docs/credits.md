# Beltbound asset credits

This page records the third-party art and fonts used in Beltbound.

- **The Ground** by **Backterria**. Adventure terrain sheet (version 1.1) and Roguelike and floor tilesheet (version 2.6). The creator permits use in free and commercial projects and allows modifications. Both original sheets and the brighter palette variant of the Adventure sheet are installed locally and excluded from Git. [Creator page and license](https://backterria.itch.io/the-ground)
- **UI Pack - Pixel Adventure** by **Kenney**. Pixel-art UI sprites released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. [Official pack and license](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack-pixel-adventure)
- **Tuffy Regular 1.1** by **Thatcher Ulrich**. Public domain font used in the debug overlay. [Font source and license notice](../assets/fonts/README.md)

For each new asset, record its name, creator, where it appears in the game, and a link to the original license. Keep any required attribution wording with the entry.
